The Programs

Start anywhere. Most people find one thing that fits, then work their way into the rest.

TAP Talks

Live sessions, seasonal episodes

Virtual and hybrid financial education sessions that run in seasonal episodes. Each one takes a single money topic and breaks it all the way down, from the hurdle that keeps people stuck to the one move you can make this week.

  • Episodes tiered by where you actually are, so nothing goes over your head
  • Every session covers the wealth anchor, the most important thing, and a direct action
  • Recent topics: credit and debt, buying a car, career strategy
See the lineup Episode updates

TAP Guides

Interactive tools and reads

The hub you are on right now. Calculators that run your real numbers, guidance sorted by wealth stage, and plain language guides for students, young professionals, and parents.

  • Net worth calculator that places you in one of five wealth stages
  • Tools for rent vs. buy, debt vs. investing, budgeting, and car affordability
  • Guides on benefits, career moves, internships, and family money talks

TAP Legacy Scholarship

For New York high school seniors

An essay based award for New York high school seniors heading into higher education. No application fee. Write about how you plan to build something that outlasts you, and let the work speak.

  • Open to NY high school seniors continuing on to higher education
  • Essay based and reviewed by the TAP team
  • Funds go straight toward the cost of school

The 5 Wealth Stages

Everything we teach is sorted by stage, so the advice you get matches the position you are actually in.

Emerging Negative to $0
Rooted $1 to $49K
Progressing $50K to $199K
Aligned $200K to $499K
Affluent $500K+
Not a ranking. A starting point.

Run your numbers in the net worth calculator and the hub will point you to the guides built for your stage.
