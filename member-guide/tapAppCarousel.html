Set up

Sign up in a minute

No password to remember. Just your email and a 6-digit code.

  1. Open the app and tap Get started.
  2. Enter your email and tap Email me a code.
  3. Type the code from your inbox, then agree to how TAP keeps your information.

Set up

A few quick questions

Your answers shape the guides and next steps we suggest.

  1. Tap the answer that fits. It moves on by itself.
  2. Pick anything big coming up, like a move or a new car.
  3. Skip what you’d rather not say. Change answers any time in Me.

Set up

Add what you own and owe

Today’s balances set your starting net worth. Round numbers are fine.

  1. Enter each account’s Balance today.
  2. Add what you owe: cards, loans, anything else.
  3. Tap See my stage.

Had a Clarity Session? The number your host recorded is ready to use.

Set up

Check your stage milestones

Six per stage. They get you ready to hold the next one.

  1. Answer Yes, done or Not yet for each one.
  2. Find them any time under Journey.
  3. Tap a milestone to see why it matters.

Day to day

Home shows your next step

Open the app and know where you stand and what to do next.

  1. See your stage and how far to the next.
  2. Do the action from your session, or tap Not now.
  3. See when your next Clarity Session is.

Day to day

Never miss what’s on

Events, new features and news from TAP slide by at the top of Home.

  1. Let the news slide by, or swipe through it yourself.
  2. Touch the card to hold it still.
  3. Tap a button to save your spot or try what’s new. A ↗ opens a web page.

Day to day

See where it’s all heading

Your Wealth Map turns today’s numbers into a forecast, down to the month you’d reach each stage.

  1. Open Map.
  2. Switch a plan on or off, like a trip or a raise, and watch the difference.
  3. Tap or drag across a chart to move through time.

Day to day

Leave every session with a plan

After a free Clarity Session with a TAP host, your recap is waiting in the app.

  1. Open Sessions, then your recap.
  2. See your Wealth Anchor and the one Most Important Thing.
  3. Work through your actions before each due date.

Day to day

Check in before the call

Tell your host how it went, so your time together goes to what’s next.

  1. On Home, tap Answer your check-in early.
  2. Share what got done and what got in the way.
  3. Tap Send. Your host sees it before you meet.

Day to day

Big decisions, step by step

Buying a car or a home, renting, having a child: guided steps with calculators that use your numbers.

  1. Open Paths and pick one.
  2. Go one step at a time. Your progress is saved.
  3. Talk it through with your host before you sign.

Once a month

A few minutes a month

Update your balances, and your net worth, stage and forecast update with them.

  1. Open Map and tap Edit my map.
  2. Update each Balance today.
  3. Tap Done. That’s it.

Always

Your data, your call

You choose who sees your Wealth Map, and you can take your data with you.

  1. On Map, tap Change under the title.
  2. Share with your host, or make it fully private.
  3. In Me, download your data or delete your account.
