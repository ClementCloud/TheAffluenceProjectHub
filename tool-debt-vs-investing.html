Strategies we compare

Pay debt first

Avalanche: put your extra toward the highest-rate debt, then roll freed minimums into the next. After debt is gone, invest everything you were paying toward loans.

Invest while paying minimums

Pay only minimums on debt and invest your extra from month one. Debts take longer; your portfolio starts growing immediately.

After the last debt is paid under either plan, we assume you invest the full amount you had been putting toward debt (all former minimums plus your extra) each month until the comparison date.
